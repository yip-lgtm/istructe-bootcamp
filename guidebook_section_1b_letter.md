## p.92 Scheme Recommendation Example 1 — RC Transfer vs Steel Hanger

### 對比表格

| Aspect | Scheme 1 – R.C. Scheme Transfer | Scheme 2 – Steel Hanger |
|--------|--------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Superstructure | RC frame + steel transfer truss below Level 2 | Steel frame + steel transfer truss on roof + hangers in tension |
| Site Construction | Ready-mix preferred, easier logistic planning | Members need delivery, traffic + crane planning needed |
| Programme | Shorter lead times, material + skills readily available | Longer lead times, skills are scarce |
| Economy | Cheap to source + skills in abundance | Limited sizes, expensive, no adequate skills to fire-proof |
| Environmental | Higher thermal mass provides stable internal environment | Lower carbon footprint |
| Health and Safety | Easy to manage & construct. Fire design easier. Plenty contractors | More effort to fire protect. Too many moving elements over worker |

### 結論
> Scheme 1 offers less risk regarding health and safety. Fire protection is easier and there are plenty skilled contractors and raw material to safely erect it. Based on these reasons, I recommend scheme 1.

### English（考官版）
> **Scheme 1 – R.C. Scheme Transfer**
> RC frame with steel transfer truss below level 2. Loads from LV 5–2 transferred by gravity. Ready-mix preferred to minimize pollution and noise. Shorter lead times + material + skills readily available. Cheap to source + skills in abundance. Higher thermal mass provides stable internal environment. Easy to manage & construct. Fire design easier to comply with.
>
> **Scheme 2 – Steel Hanger**
> Steel frame with steel transfer truss on the roof. Hangers in tension transfer storey load to roof truss. Members need to be delivered to site. Traffic accommodation must be planned. Longer lead times, skills are scarce. Limited sizes available + expensive to source. No adequate skills to fire-proof. Lower carbon footprint. More effort to fire protect. Too many moving elements over worker during erection.

### Distinction 寫法
- 每一個 Aspect 都要有 優點 + 缺點 (balanced discussion)
- 最後結論要 清晰 + 有理由
- 強調 H&S + Fire Protection + Local Skills Availability 適合 civic / public building

## p.93 Scheme Recommendation Example 2 — RC Flatslab vs Hollowcore + RC Beam

### Scheme 1 – R.C. Flatslab (明顯勝出)
**優點 (+):**
- (+) Less offsite works
- (+) Less sub-contractor involved
- (+) Lower cost due to use of more insitu concrete
- (+) Technical familiar to local worker
- (+) Less crane requirements
- (+) Can make modifications on site more easily
- (+) Flat soffit, better for services

### Scheme 2 – Hollowcore + R.C. Beam
**優點 (+):**
- (+) Less manpower required on site → Improve safety
- (+) Lighter, more efficient structure system
- (+) Less material usage, more sustainable

### 結論
> Based on the above comparison of two options, I recommend to proceed with option 1 due to the expected lower cost and use of technology familiar with the local market.

### English（考官版）
> **Scheme 1 – R.C. Flatslab:** Less offsite works. Less sub-contractor involved. Lower cost due to use of more insitu concrete. Technical familiar to local worker. Less crane requirements. Can make modifications on site more easily. Flat soffit, better for services.
>
> **Scheme 2 – Hollowcore + R.C. Beam:** Less manpower required on site → Improve safety. Lighter, more efficient structure system. Less material usage, more sustainable.

### Distinction 技巧
- **Example 1 風格：** 詳細比較每一個 Aspect (適合複雜項目)
- **Example 2 風格：** Scheme 1 寫得多啲優點，Scheme 2 寫少啲，結論清晰 (適合簡單項目)

### 9 大策略 (Logical Thinking Path)

| Strategy | 描述 |
|----------|------|
| 1. Relocating mechanical plant | 將機電設備搬去 basement，減少上層荷載 |
| 2. Reducing floor-to-floor heights | 減少樓層高度，將 MEP 整合入結構 zone |
| 3. Relocating cores | 重新安排 core 位置，提升效率 |
| 4. Relaxing column spacing restrictions | 放寬柱距限制 |
| 5. Allowing bracing in external façade | 容許外牆 bracing |
| 6. Removal / reduction of cantilevered section | 減少或取消 cantilever |
| 7. Removal of basement | 取消 basement (如果可行) |
| 8. Reducing imposed loads | 減少 imposed load |
| 9. Adding beams in no-floor zone | 喺無樓板區域加 beam，減少 column 同 mullion size |

### English（考官版）
> **REDUCE THE MATERIAL USE WHILE MAINTAINING THE FLOOR PLAN AND NUMBER OF STOREYS**
> 
> **Logical thinking path**
> 
> Possible options were:
> - Relocating the mechanical plant to the basement to avoid heavy loads at upper levels
> - Reducing floor-to-floor heights by integrating the mechanical zone with the structural zone
> - Relocating cores for greater efficiency
> - Relaxing column spacing restrictions
> - Allowing bracing in the external façade
> - Removal / reduction of the cantilevered section of the building subject to the client's approval
> - Removal of the basement subject to the client's approval
> - Reducing imposed loads to reduce the overall load on the building
> - Adding beams between columns in the no-floor zone to reduce size of the columns and the mullions

## p.97 Case 1 & Case 2 (Material Reduction Examples)

### Case 1 – Transfer Requirement
**PROPOSAL: INTRODUCE STRATEGIC COLUMNS IN SHADED CLEAR-SPAN AREA**

| Stage | 內容 |
|-------|------|
| **Current Issue** | Shaded area 要求 column-free (Level 1–3) → Level 4 有 massive transfer structure. Transfer beams span 16–24m, depth 1,500–2,200mm. 佔總 structural material 35–40% |
| **Proposed Change** | 加 2–4 條 slender columns (300–400mm diameter) 喺 shaded area. Strategically positioned 去 support Level 4 structure |
| **Benefits** | (1) Eliminates heavy transfer structure (2,200mm → 600mm) (2) Reduces transfer spans by 50% (16–24m → 8–12m) (3) Foundation sizes reduced by 30–40% (4) Columns can be architectural features (5) Maintains open, flexible layouts |

### Case 2 – Optimise Floor-to-Floor Heights
**PROPOSAL: OPTIMIZE FLOOR-TO-CEILING HEIGHTS (DIFFERENTIAL APPROACH)**

| Stage | 內容 |
|-------|------|
| **Current Issue** | 2.70m clear + 350mm services = 3.40m floor-to-floor (全部樓層一樣) |
| **Proposed Change** | (1) Level 1–3 (public/conference): 維持 2.70m clear (civic presence) (2) Level 4–7 (offices): 減到 2.60m clear (超過 BCO minimum 2.55m) (3) Services zone: 350mm → 250mm (integrate MEP within structural depth) (4) Height saving: 100mm × 4 floors = 400mm |
| **Benefits** | (1) Optimizes material within fixed height constraint (2) Reduced structural zone using post-tensioned slabs (3) Lower wind loads (8–10% reduction) (4) Vertical elements saving (5) 2.60m office height imperceptible to occupants |

### Polished Exam-Ready Version (Law Court Q1 Adapted)

**PROPOSAL 1: INTRODUCE STRATEGIC COLUMNS TO ELIMINATE HEAVY TRANSFER STRUCTURE AT THE ATRIUM**

> **Current Issue:** The requirement for a column-free 2-storey atrium at the north elevation necessitates a substantial transfer structure at Level 3. The transfer beams spanning the atrium would be 16–20m long with depths up to 2,000mm+, representing a significant proportion of the total structural material and foundation demand.
>
> **Proposed Change:** It is proposed to introduce 3–4 slender internal columns (350mm diameter) within the atrium zone at Levels 1–2, strategically positioned to support the Level 3 transfer structure while minimising impact on the column-free spatial requirement.
>
> **Benefits:**
> - Eliminates the need for heavy transfer beams (depth reduced from ~2,000mm to 600mm typical).
> - Reduces transfer spans by approximately 50%, significantly improving structural efficiency.
> - Achieves 30–35% reduction in foundation sizes due to more direct load paths.
> - The new columns can be architecturally expressed as feature elements within the atrium.
> - Maintains the required column-free atrium experience with minimal visual impact.

**PROPOSAL 2: OPTIMISE FLOOR-TO-FLOOR HEIGHTS USING A DIFFERENTIAL APPROACH**

> **Current Issue:** A uniform floor-to-floor height of 3.40m is proposed across all levels. This conservative approach leads to unnecessary material usage and increased wind loading on the 10-storey building.
>
> **Proposed Change:** A differential floor-to-floor strategy is proposed:
> - Levels 1–3 (public and court areas): Maintain 2.75m clear height to preserve the civic and formal character.
> - Levels 4–10 (office and support areas): Reduce clear height to 2.65m (exceeds BCO minimum).
> - Services zone: Reduce from 300mm to 250mm through integration with post-tensioned flat slabs.
>
> **Benefits:**
> - Achieves approximately 350–400mm total height saving.
> - Reduces wind loads by 8–10%, resulting in smaller column and foundation sizes.
> - Enables more efficient use of post-tensioned flat slabs throughout the typical floors.
> - The reduction in office clear height remains imperceptible and fully compliant with modern standards.

## p.98 Case 3 & Case 4 (Material Reduction)

### Case 3 – Permit Strategic Shear Walls at Building Perimeter

| Stage | 內容 |
|-------|------|
| **Current Issue** | 客戶要求「除核心牆外不得設置剪力牆」, 導致必須採用 moment-resisting frame. Moment frame 因連接複雜及構件較大, 會造成 20–30% 額外材料用量 |
| **Proposed Change** | 方案 A (推薦): 於建築物南北兩端各設兩道 6–8 米長 RC 剪力牆. 方案 B: 於四個轉角各設 3–4 米長剪力牆. 牆身厚度 250–300 毫米, 與外牆立面融合 |
| **Benefits** | (1) 取消 moment frame, 改用 pinned connections (鋼筋用量減少 50–60%) (2) 柱截面由 600×600mm 減至 450×450mm (節省 44% 混凝土) (3) 梁截面由 600×800mm 減至 500×650mm (節省 28% 混凝土) (4) 基礎彎矩減少 60%, 基礎尺寸可減少 20–25% (5) 提升側向剛度, 減少側移 (6) 零視覺影響 (7) 可保留開窗 (採用 coupled wall 設計) |

### Case 4 – Rationalize Plant Room Column Arrangement

| Stage | 內容 |
|-------|------|
| **Current Issue** | 設備層 (Level 4) 限制「內柱不多於 6 支, 最小柱距 9.0 米」, 導致長跨 roof structure (梁/桁架深達 1,200mm) 及大型柱截面 (500×500mm) |
| **Proposed Change** | 將內柱由 6 支增加至 10–12 支. 柱距減至 6–7 米. 維持設備佈局靈活性 |
| **Benefits** | (1) Roof beam 跨度由 9 米減至 6–7 米 (減少 22–33%) (2) Roof beam 深度由 1,200mm 減至 700mm (減少 42%) (3) 柱截面由 500×500mm 減至 400×400mm (混凝土減少 36%) (4) Roof 結構變輕, 連帶減輕下層所有構件荷載 |

### English（考官版）

> **Case 3 – Permit Strategic Shear Walls at Building Perimeter**
> 
> **Current Issue:** The requirement of "no shear walls except around cores" forces the use of moment-resisting frames, which carry a 20–30% material penalty due to heavy connections and large member sizes.
> 
> **Proposed Change:** Introduce discrete RC shear walls at the building perimeter:
> - Option A (Preferred): Two 6–8m long walls at north and south ends
> - Option B: Four 3–4m corner walls
> - Wall thickness 250–300mm, fully integrated within the facade
> 
> **Benefits:**
> - Eliminates moment frame → pinned connections (50–60% less reinforcement)
> - Column size reduction: 600×600mm → 450×450mm (44% saving)
> - Beam size reduction: 600×800mm → 500×650mm (28% saving)
> - Foundation moments reduced by 60% → 20–25% smaller foundations
> - Enhanced lateral stiffness and reduced drift
> - Zero visual impact (integrated within facade)
> - Can accommodate windows (coupled wall design)
> 
> **Case 4 – Rationalize Plant Room Column Arrangement**
> 
> **Current Issue:** Restriction of maximum 6 internal columns with minimum 9.0m spacing at plant room level results in long-span roof structure (1,200mm deep beams/trusses) and large column sections (500×500mm).
> 
> **Proposed Change:** Increase internal columns from 6 to 10–12 nos. and reduce spacing to 6–7m grid while maintaining plant layout flexibility.
> 
> **Benefits:**
> - Roof beam spans reduced from 9m to 6–7m (22–33% reduction)
> - Roof beam depth reduced from 1,200mm to 700mm (42% reduction)
> - Column sizes reduced from 500×500mm to 400×400mm (36% less concrete)
> - Lighter roof structure reduces loads on all elements below
