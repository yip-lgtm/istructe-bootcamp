## 5. Recommendation

**Recommended Scheme:** Scheme 1 RC PT Flat Slab

**Justification:** Superior whole-life performance for occupied Law Court (thermal mass reduces operational carbon 15–18%). Bored piles support circular economy in city centre. Minor whole-life advantage to Steel is offset by higher long-term HVAC demand and maintenance.

**Examiner Note:** Always declare assumptions (GGBS %, recycled rate, thermal mass saving %) and link to client requirements (occupied building, 60-year design life, city centre constraints).
