# Major Non-Compliance Checklist — IStructE CM Exam

Project: July 2025 Q1 Law Court 
Date: 8 May 2026 
Reference: GUIDEBOOK p.12–14 + Examiner Reports 2024–2025

## Critical Major Non-Compliances (Automatic Fail — 10 marks deduction each)

| # | Non-Compliance | Law Court Specific Risk | How to Avoid |
|---|---------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Column in column-free courtroom zone | 10-storey Law Court — court spaces must be column-free | Perimeter columns only, min 5.0m spacing, transfer structure at pods |
| 2 | Bracing to north glazed atrium | Client non-negotiable — no bracing permitted | All lateral via cores A + B only; declare "no bracing to north" in every scheme |
| 3 | Missing load path diagram | Examiner Report July 2025 — common fail | Always draw gravity + lateral load path arrows on every sketch |
| 4 | Foundation not distinct between schemes | Bored piles vs socketed H-piles must be clearly different | Separate table + small sketch for each scheme |
| 5 | No sustainability discriminator | 2024+ IStructE requirement — whole-life carbon | Always include 200-word carbon comparison + clear recommendation |
| 6 | Inverted structure / unrealistic spans | 10.5m bay with 300mm slab — check deflection | Span/depth ratio ≤35 for PT flat slab; show calculation |
| 7 | Wind load assumption not declared | V_s = 23 m/s mean hourly must be stated | Write "V_s = 23 m/s (46 m/s gust per BS EN 1991-1-4)" in assumptions |
| 8 | No fire resistance strategy | 120 min required for all elements | State cover 35mm + fire rating table reference |

## Minor Non-Compliances (5 marks deduction)

- Missing deflection limits (span/250, span/360)
- No vibration check (>4 Hz)
- Incomplete foundation table (missing N value, GWL, founding level)
- No circular economy note (GGBS, recycled steel, bored piles)

## Examiner Tip (July 2025 Report)

"Candidates who clearly stated assumptions, showed distinct schemes, and included a sustainability discriminator scored 9.5+ on Section 1(a). Those who missed load path or put columns in court spaces failed."

## 5. Recommendation (Section 1(a) Closure)

**Recommended Scheme:** Scheme 1 RC PT Flat Slab

**Justification:** Superior whole-life performance for occupied Law Court (thermal mass reduces operational carbon 15–18%). Bored piles support circular economy in city centre. Minor whole-life advantage to Steel is offset by higher long-term HVAC demand and maintenance.

**Examiner Note:** Always declare assumptions (GGBS %, recycled rate, thermal mass saving %) and link to client requirements (occupied building, 60-year design life, city centre constraints).
