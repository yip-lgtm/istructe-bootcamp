# IStructE CM Exam — July 2025 Q1 Law Court Brief

Project: Multi-storey Law Court Building 
Location: City centre site (70m x 30m) 
Client Requirements:
- 10-storey law court building
- Column-free courtrooms (min 5.0m column spacing)
- 2-storey north atrium void (15m x 15m, fully glazed, no bracing permitted)
- East and West meeting room pods
- Floor-to-floor height: 3.05m (2.75m clear + 300mm services)
- Roof plant area: 7.5 kN/m²
- Fire resistance: 120 minutes
- Design life: 60 years

Site Conditions:
- Ground: 0.0–0.5m made ground; 0.5–15m sandy gravel (N=45); GWL at –2.0m AOD
- Wind: 46 m/s 3-sec gust (V_s = 23 m/s mean hourly per BS EN 1991-1-4)
- No basement permitted

Sustainability Targets:
- Whole-life carbon reduction
- Circular economy principles
- Material efficiency (GGBS, recycled aggregates, recycled steel)

Key Constraints:
- No internal columns in court spaces
- No bracing to north glazed atrium
- City centre noise/vibration restrictions
- Major Non-compliance if column in column-free zone or missing load path

Exam Question Focus:
- Two distinct viable structural schemes (RC vs Steel)
- Section 1(a) Design Appraisal + Recommendations
- Section 1(b) Client Letter (if required)
- Section 2(c) Calculations (slab, beam, column, foundation, carbon)
- Section 2(d) Drawings (framing plan + details)
- Section 2(e) Method Statement + Programme
