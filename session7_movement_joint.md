# Session 7 Supplement — Movement Joint + Separated Core
## GUIDEBOOK p.41 — § Other Considerations
**Date:** 2026-05-11 (Monday) —补充 Session 7

---

## 中文詳解

如果出現以下情況（Contains movement joint），整個建築會因引入伸縮縫（movement joint）而被橫向分開，左右兩邊建築物會獨立抵抗側向荷載。

建築物會在基礎以上被伸縮縫分割成兩個獨立結構，伸缩缝位置位於建築物中心（E-W 方向）。每邊結構的牆壁會獨立承受側向荷載。

### Case 1 — Separated Core（分離核心）
會使用鋼筋混凝土剪力牆（shear wall）包圍電梯及樓梯核心，用來穩定建築物抵抗側向荷載。

## 英文詳解（考官最愛版本，直接抄落 A4 筆記）

> § Other Considerations (if the following situation appears)
>
> (Contains movement joint) Since the whole building will be laterally separated by introducing a movement joint, the two buildings will resist lateral load independently.
>
> The building will be split into separate structures above foundation by a movement joint across the center of the building in the E-W direction.
>
> Each section on either side of the building wall acts independently under lateral loads.
>
> (Case 1 – Separated core)
>
> Reinforced concrete shear walls will be used around the elevator and stair-cores to stabilise the building under lateral loads.

```

   ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
   │                 │                 │
   │   Structure 1   │   Structure 2   │
   │    (Core 1)     │    (Core 3)     │
   │                 │ M.J.           │
   ├────Shear Wall 1─┼─────────────────┤
   │                 │                 │
   │   (Core 2)       │   (Core 4)     │
   │                 │                 │
   └─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

- 建築物被 M.J.（Movement Joint）分開成 Structure 1（左）+ Structure 2（右）
- Core 1、Core 2、Core 3、Core 4 分別位於兩邊
- Shear Wall 1 + Shear Wall 2 提供 lateral resistance
- Wind X（水平方向）及 Wind Y（垂直方向）由各自核心及剪力牆獨立抵抗

## Sustainability Link（Distinction 必加）

Movement joint 令兩個結構獨立工作，可減少整體剛度，從而降低材料用量及 embodied carbon。同時方便未來維修及拆除，符合 circular economy 原則。

---

## Key Points for Exam

1. **Movement Joint** = 建築物被分割成兩個獨立結構
2. **Each side acts independently** under lateral loads
3. **Shear walls** stabilise each section
4. **Sustainability benefit** = reduced material, easier maintenance/demolition
