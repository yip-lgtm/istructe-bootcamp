## 🌀 BLOCK 2 — Wind Load Calculation

### Key Assumptions (Law Court)
- V_s = 23 m/s (mean hourly wind speed)
- From 46 m/s 3-sec gust per BS EN 1991-1-4
- Building height H = 35 m (10-storey @ 3.5m/floor)
- Plan: 70m × 30m

### Wind Pressure Formula
```
q = 0.613 × V_s² (kN/m²)
q = 0.613 × 23² = 0.613 × 529 = 0.325 kN/m²
```

### External Pressure Coefficients (c_e)
- Terrain Category 2 (city centre)
- c_e ≈ 2.0 (at H = 35m)

### Design Wind Pressure
```
p = q × c_e × c_pg
p = 0.325 × 2.0 × 1.0 ≈ 0.65 kN/m² (net)
```
