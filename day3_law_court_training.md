## BLOCK 1

### 3' INTRODUCTION — Law Court

The Law Court building comprises a public atrium hall, multiple courtrooms of varying spans, and ancillary accommodation arranged around a central core. The structural challenge is to transfer loads from the courtrooms and public spaces while maintaining flexibility for future layout changes, all within a tight programme and budget.

The primary structural system comprises reinforced concrete flat slabs supported on a central core, perimeter columns, and transfer structures at roof level, with an open atrium that reduces the slab span and introduces a structural podium level.

## BLOCK 2

### 5' SCHEME 1 RC Sketch + Load Transfer Path

**Scheme 1: RC Flat Slab + Central Core + Atrium + Structural Podium**

**Description:**
A reinforced concrete flat slab (300mm typical thickness) spans between perimeter columns (600×600mm) and a central reinforced concrete core (500mm thick walls). The core contains lift shafts, stairwells, and service ducts, providing both gravity and lateral load resistance. An open atrium (approximately 12m × 18m) at the lower ground floor removes a section of slab, creating a double-height public space. A structural podium at +1F level carries the courtrooms above and transfers loads to the core and perimeter columns.

**Load Transfer Path — Gravity:**

1. **Courtroom loads (upper floors):** Slab (300mm RC, LL ~4–6 kN/m²) → drop panels (400mm thick at columns) → RC columns (600×600) → RC core walls (500mm)
2. **Atrium void:** Load from slab areas surrounding atrium is directed around the void via slab spanning action (effective depth maintained by 300mm slab + 100mm drop)
3. **Podium level (+1F):** Transfer beams at podium (500mm × 900mm) carry upper courtrooms → distribute to core perimeter and columns below atrium zone
4. **Central core:** All column loads from upper floors are collected in the core at each floor and transferred to the foundation via a pile cap system (raft where ground conditions permit)
5. **Foundation:** RC pad foundations under core walls; pile groups under perimeter columns if ground is soft

**Load Transfer Path — Lateral (Wind/Seismic):**

- Lateral loads → floor diaphragms (300mm RC slab acts as rigid diaphragm) → core walls (500mm) → foundation
- Outrigger beams at roof level (if needed) connect core to perimeter columns for enhanced stiffness
- Atrium void creates a discontinuity in the diaphragm; additional steel bracing or thickening of slab edges around void required

**Schematic Section Levels:**
- **+12.000:** Roof – flat slab (300mm) + plant
- **+9.000:** Courtroom 3 – flat slab (300mm)
- **+6.000:** Courtroom 2 – flat slab (300mm) + atrium edge beam
- **+3.000:** Courtroom 1 / public corridor – flat slab (300mm) + drop panels
- **±0.000:** Ground floor – raft foundation + atrium void (12m × 18m opening)
- **-3.000:** Basement – pile caps + service basement

**Key Notes:**
- The atrium reduces overall slab quantity but introduces stress concentrations at the void edges; additional reinforcement required (crank bars, hairpin bars)
- Core walls should be continuous from foundation to roof to avoid soft-storey conditions
- Drop panel depth: 400mm (100mm increase over slab) to resist punching shear at column locations

### Major Non-Compliance Check

**Likely Non-Compliances for Each Scheme:**

| Scheme | Issue | Code Reference | Risk | Mitigation |
|--------|-------|----------------|------|------------|
| S1 RC Flat Slab | Punching shear at column-slab connection (no drop panels in some areas) | BS EN 1992-1-1 Cl. 6.4 | High | Add drop panels; increase slab thickness at columns |
| S2 Steel Truss | Fire resistance of steel members (courtrooms require 120-min rating) | BS 9999 / BS EN 1993-1-2 | High | Intumescent coating (60 min dry) or concrete encasement |
| S3 PT Flat Slab | Tendon corrosion risk in post-tensioned slab (waterproofing critical) | BS EN 1992-1-1 Cl. 4.4 | Medium | Grouted tendons + membrane waterproofing; monitoring |
| S4 Composite Steel | Vibration performance under human activity (courtrooms sensitive) | BS EN 1993-1-1 Cl. NA.2.8 | Medium | Add concrete topping; limit span-to-depth ratio |
| S5 RC + Outriggers | Soft storey at podium level (atrium creates discontinuity) | BS EN 1998-1 Cl. 4.4.2.2 | High | RC walls at podium perimeter; stiffness check required |
| S6 Transfer Plate | Long-term deflection of transfer plate (creep + shrinkage) | BS EN 1992-1-1 Cl. 7.4 | High | Prestress the plate; specify low-shrinkage concrete |
| S7 Hybrid | Connection detailing between steel and RC (construction tolerance) | BS EN 1993-1-8 | Medium | Grouted sleeves; site tolerance protocol |
| S8 RC Waffle | Shallow rib depth may not meet punching shear requirements | BS EN 1992-1-1 Cl. 6.4 | High | Thicken slab; add solid areas at columns |

**Universal Non-Compliances to Address for Law Court:**
1. **Acoustic separation:** Floor slabs require minimum 150mm thickness or acoustic floating floor to meet courtroom sound isolation requirements (HTM for court buildings)
2. **Accessibility:** No step changes >15mm at door thresholds; slab penetrations for M&E must not reduce structural capacity below required levels
3. **Fire engineering:** All escape routes through atrium must be protected; structural elements must achieve required fire rating (typically 120 min for loadbearing elements)
4. **Movement joints:** Long building plan dimension (>50m) requires movement joints every ~30m to accommodate thermal and shrinkage movement
