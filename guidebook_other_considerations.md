## 1. (façade / curtain wall / cladding) Set back of external wall / columns

**中文：** 外牆 / 幕牆 / 外飾面需要後退柱線（例如後退 150 mm / 250 mm），以安裝幕牆系統。

**English（考官版）：**
Set back of external wall / columns for installation of façade / curtain walls / external cladding (say 150 / 250 mm).

**考試用法：** 在 Scheme 1 / 2 圖中標註 "External column set back 150 mm for curtain wall"

---

## 2. (Movement Joint Reqd) Provision of movement joint (MJ)

**中文：**

**(Not Required)：** 低層、小面積、規則形狀建築物，因溫度及收縮引起的變形不大，無需伸縮縫。

**(Required)：**
- a) 建築物總長 > 50 m → 每兩個結構區之間設 25 mm MJ
- b) 地下室 → 減低不均勻水壓上浮
- c) L 形或不規則形狀 → 減低扭矩及裂縫
- d) 兩個結構區只靠小連桿連接 → 弱連接處需設 MJ
- e) 靠近現有建築物 → 防止不均勻沉降

**English（考官版）：**

(Not Required) For low rise building with small footprint, joints are not required.

(Required)
- a) Maximum length of building > 50 m, 25 mm MJ between 2 building zones shall be provided to reduce cracks and stress concentration due to temperature and shrinkage effect.
- b) For basement, MJ is proposed to be provided to reduce uneven uplifting water pressure induced.
- c) Since the building is "L" shape, twisting / torsion will occur due to lateral load at different location… MJ is proposed to reduce the torsion stress and avoid cracks.
- d) Since building consists of 2 building zone and linked by small link path only, crack and stress will induce at weak links… MJ is proposed to be provided between 2 building zone.
- e) Movement joints should be provided near the existing structure.

## 3. (Long Span Structure) Deflection of transfer / cantilever beam may be significant

**中文：** 長跨結構（transfer beam / cantilever beam）撓度可能很大，需考慮預拱（pre-cambering）。

**English：** Deflection of transfer / cantilever beam may be significant. Pre-cambering of certain amount may be required to reduce the amount of deflection.

---

## 4. (Wind Load Control) Top drift of tall building

**中文：** 高層建築頂部漂移（top drift）須控制在 H/500 以內，層間漂移 H/400 以內，並按 CoP 2013 檢查人體舒適度加速度。

**English：** Top drift < H / 500 shall be checked and inter-storey drift < H / 400 to be checked. Acceleration of building motion to be checked in accordance to CoP 2013 for Human Comfort Criteria.

---

## 5. (Asymmetrical core) Torsional Moment on Floor

**中文：** 核心不對稱排列，風荷載會產生扭矩（torsional moment），需由 RC 框架 + 核心共同抵抗。

**English：** Torsional moment on floor will be induced by wind load due to asymmetrical arrangement of the core wall. Torsional moment induced by wind will be resisted by the R.C. frame and Core Wall. It will be checked in detail design stage.

---

## 6. (Inclined Column) Inclined column at front elevation

**中文：** 前立面斜柱會在梁 / 板產生額外軸力（compression / tension），需在詳細設計階段檢查。

**English：** Axial force on inclined column will induce compression / tension force on the beam / slab connect to them. This effect will be considered in detail design stage, in addition, bending moment will be induced onto the column due to its inclined profile.

---

## 7. (Atrium Glazing) Atrium Glazing (Roof)

**中文：** 中庭玻璃屋頂需用鋼梁 + 鋼支架支撐。

**English：** Steel beams spanning between columns on roof floor will be used to support the roof glazing.（圖中顯示典型鋼框架 + 玻璃面板排列）

---

## 8. (Steel Truss) Steel Roof Truss

**中文：** 鋼屋架一端需設滾軸支承（roller support），容許溫度伸縮。

**English：** Roller support to be provided at one end to allow movement due to thermal effects.
