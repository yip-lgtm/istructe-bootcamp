### RECOMMENDATIONS (200 words)

Scheme 1 (RC post-tensioned flat slab) is recommended. Although Scheme 2 has slightly lower initial embodied carbon, the superior thermal mass of RC reduces operational carbon by ~15% over the 60-year design life of this continuously occupied Law Court. Post-tensioning and 20% GGBS replacement achieve material efficiency (18% less concrete volume), while bored piles support circular economy by minimising temporary works waste and site disturbance in the city centre.

Steel Scheme 2 offers faster dry assembly and higher recycled content, but lower thermal mass increases long-term HVAC demand and requires intumescent coating for fire resistance, adding maintenance cost. On whole-life carbon basis, RC Scheme 1 delivers better sustainability performance, robustness and compliance with IStructE emphasis on net-zero targets.
